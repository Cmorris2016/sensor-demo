Learn Drums

A single-page drum machine that teaches you rhythm, timing & common patterns. Click pads or use keyboard.

How to use: Click the pads or press the keys shown. Build beats in the 16-step sequencer below. Load a lesson from the right panel to hear classic patterns. Watch the moving highlight — that’s the pulse. Try to feel the “1” of each bar.
KICK 1 / Q Low boom. Foundation of almost every beat.
SNARE 2 / W Sharp crack. Usually hits on 2 & 4 in rock.
CLOSED HAT 3 / E Tight “tick”. Keeps time between the big hits.
OPEN HAT 4 / R Sizzling open sound. Use sparingly for accents.
TOM 5 / T Mid drum. Great for fills and transitions.
CLAP 6 / Y Hand-clap texture. Common in hip-hop & house.

Lessons & Patterns

Click any pattern to load it into the sequencer. Then press Play and listen. Try changing one or two hits to hear how the feel changes.

1. Basic Rock (Boom-Chick)

Kick on 1 & 3, Snare on 2 & 4. The classic “four-on-the-floor” backbone of rock & pop.

2. Hip-Hop Groove

Kick on 1 and the “and” of 2. Snare on 2 & 4. Slightly swung feel. Very common in 90s–2000s beats.

3. Four-on-the-Floor (House)

Kick on every beat (1-2-3-4). Open hat on the off-beats. Dance music foundation.

4. Disco / Funk

Kick on 1 & 3, Snare on 2 & 4, busy closed hats. Add open hat for sparkle.

5. Empty Canvas

Clear the grid and build your own. Start with just kick + snare, then add hats.

Pro tip: Count out loud: “1-and-2-and-3-and-4-and”. Each step is an “and”. The red highlight shows where you are in the bar. Practice playing pads in time with the sequencer — that’s how real drummers lock in.
